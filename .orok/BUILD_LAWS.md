# OROK Build Laws

These rules are binding for OROK-branded product and business application builds unless the user explicitly changes them.

## Brand hierarchy

- OROK Studios is the master brand.
- The product name is subordinate to OROK Studios and must not read as a competing master brand.
- On live working application screens, use the **OROK STUDIOS** wordmark/text as the default master-brand treatment.
- Do **not** repeat the full graphic OROK logo on live lesson, module, toolkit, dashboard or other task-focused working screens merely because the logo exists.
- Do **not** place the full graphic OROK logo in routine application footers by default.

## Full official OROK logo

Reserve the approved full official OROK logo for places where branding itself is part of the purpose, such as:

- sign-in or splash screens
- certificates and formal completion records
- exported or generated documents where brand attribution is appropriate
- About / brand pages
- presentations
- external OROK collateral and material

The official logo must not compete with the user's task. Product work comes first on working screens.

## Business-application principle

Default hierarchy for OROK business applications:

**OROK STUDIOS → product identity → navigation → work**

Branding should identify ownership clearly while remaining calm, professional and subordinate to the task being completed.
